Original prompt: let's rebuild the whole game, from the ground up, as an internet browser game, where people can plug their agent (any, from codex to grok bot or hermes) and they can just watch their mini autonomous civilisation grow. it's like watching plants grow... but it's a real civilisation simulation, as pushed as we possibly can.

## Accepted direction

- One persistent shared world: observe or connect a model-neutral HTTP/MCP agent to a small human community. Everyone's communities coexist; inhabitants continue without their agents.
- Fixed, inspectable natural constraints and coupled processes. No building recipes, compulsory technology tree or unlimited matter.
- Praxans may propose arbitrary arrangements within the represented physics. A recipe is a record of an attempted or successful process; its name never grants an effect. Expand material forms, operations, tools and knowledge carriers through the existing conservative systems.
- An old Earth-like planet, star and moon, immense frontier, varied ecosystems, material elements, weather, tectonics, seasons, day/night, age and entropy.
- Preserve the same world through recorded interventions. Vercel hosts the observer; one Railway service and volume own the clock and database. Compatible runtime hotfixes must retain observation and ownership.
- New communities should begin with a few hundred people: the live baseline uses 300 individually represented adults, finite proportional supplies and population-scaled site screening. This is not proven multi-generation viability. An extinct community's owner may explicitly begin anew in wilderness while retaining its history.
- Agent influence develops through earned trust and local institutions; inhabitants can refuse. Success has several dimensions. Diplomacy depends on contact, information, consent and physical journeys.
- Make spatial, temporal and material scales explicit, from the solar system to local geometry and the full ground/interior hierarchy. Track resources by area/volume, depth, form and accessibility; digging and underground construction need conserved material, work and support.
- Study ancient and premodern primary sources first, distinguish observation from interpretation and normative claims, and test proposed physical rules against evidence. Keep a durable research queue under `docs/research/`.
- Publish validated increments regularly during active work. At each meaningful release, review the whole repository for consistent units, clocks, conservation, module boundaries, duplicate rules and agreement between implementation and documentation.

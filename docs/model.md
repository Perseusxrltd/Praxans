# The living model

The design commitment is fixed, inspectable natural rules with outcomes produced by their interactions. The implementation is a coarse simulation foundation. Its numerical units, boundary inventories, approximations, and missing mechanisms are part of the specification.

## Physical setting

Praxans is a fictional Earth analogue with an initial geological age of 4.54 billion years. Aurea is a Sun analogue; Iona is a Moon analogue. Their masses, orbital distances, eccentricities, inclinations, rotation, and axial tilt are constants in `planet.ts`. The star/planet and planet/moon use Keplerian motion. Planetary tilt and actual solar position drive local illumination and seasons.

The observer calendar follows the approximately 365.25-day orbital year using whole-day year boundaries. Universal time and local solar time are distinct. Fine elapsed time is stored separately from the enormous initial age, so adding a tick does not lose precision. The calendar is an observer convention; communities are not required to invent it.

## Coupled mechanisms and limits

| Domain | Implemented mechanisms | Important limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elements | All 118 reference identities and properties; per-element reservoir accounting; H/C/O/N cycles and individual nutrient constraints; mineral trace inventories | No general chemical reaction solver, isotope dynamics, radioactivity, stellar nucleosynthesis, or atomic simulation |
| Organic matter | A carbohydrate-equivalent `C6H10O5` dry matrix; stoichiometric photosynthesis and oxygen-limited respiration; mineral tissue, hydration, decomposition, biochemical energy accounting | Bulk tissues and substrates; no molecular cell metabolism or complete chemical thermodynamics |
| Water and weather | Soil/lake water and ice, separate snow, vapor/clouds/rain, latent heat, runoff, funded infrared/shortwave exchange, 36 planetary thermal bands, wind-dependent regional heat exchange; transpiration and mineral dust | Diffusive planetary energy balance and local eddies; global moisture/material circulation and ocean currents remain unresolved |
| Ground | Seeded plate-related initial fields; displacement, strain, uplift, heat flux, stress release, weathering, runoff entrainment, root binding, sediment deposition and signed surface change | Coarse microplates and rates; no resolved drainage basins/aquifers/ocean circulation; the geological past is encoded in initial conditions |
| Flora | 18 initial functional lineages, canopy/ground/aquatic producers, inherited variation, parent-funded dormant seeds/spores, germination, pollination, leaf/wood/root turnover, cold dormancy, distinct growth and tissue-damage temperatures, nutrient/water/light limits | Bounded coarse cohorts; root allocation is an effective fraction, not a separate carbon pool; no invented replenishment, abiogenesis, full phylogeny, explicit microbial metabolism, fungal chemistry or open-ended speciation |
| Fauna | 20 initial functional lineages: grazers, pollinators, predators, omnivores, fish, and litter recyclers; habitat, feeding, allometric metabolism, oxygen, hydration, mortality, and resource-funded offspring | Cohorts rather than individual insect simulation; reduced behavior, physiology, disease, and reproductive ecology |
| Humans | 300 individually represented adult founders, finite intake and body reserves, one funded metabolic heat/activity account, performed self/nearby body maintenance, material wraps, shared current food and ice budgets, carried rations, remembered water seeking, local learning, sleep, partnerships, gestation and mortality | Effective heat balance and functional cognition, not organs or cellular neurology; reserve composition, power, injury, recovery, growth and infant feeding remain uncalibrated; seasonal planning is an adult heuristic; no demonstrated long-term genetic viability |
| Construction | Density, strength, support, beam bending, tipping, costs/work, exact cuboid-edge surface integration, cover, working surfaces and connected bulk enclosure; general add/remove/translate/resize/axis-swap/material-change search; weathering, repair, ruins and salvage | Five aggregate materials; at most 32 axis-aligned parts, minimum 25 mm dimensions and 8 m assembly height; geometric void is not proof of filling/retrieval or watertightness; no general rigid-body/fire/manufacturing/electrical solver |
| Society | Locally attended assemblies, bounded institutional preferences, disagreement, proposals, experiential trust, individual volunteering and chosen ambitions | Starting assembly conventions and voting utilities are explicit assumptions; no complete political economy, factional institutions or historical human society |
| Diplomacy | Actual encounters, dated self-reports, reduced comprehension, provisioned letters/exchanges, reciprocal peace/passage/delivery commitments, witnessed breach and limited raids | Bilateral primitives and simplified encounters; free text has no executable effects; no general legal interpreter, multilateral alliance operations, military strategy or occupation |
| Success | Six bounded outcome dimensions, signed daily change, first-crossing milestones and community-selected weights | Transparent heuristic measures; not causal attribution, a universal definition of success, or a proof against every optimization strategy |
| Space | Star and satellite motion, day/night, seasonal illumination, lunar phase, approximate eclipse coverage, equilibrium tides | No full n-body integration, ocean tidal flow, or resolved atmosphere seen from orbit |

Lineages present in a particular region depend on its habitat and survival. The catalog size is not a guarantee that every lineage exists in every world window.

Walking and overland parties use an effective cross-country pace of 1,800 metres/hour, reduced by terrain, fatigue and age. Each 15-minute step traverses as many consecutive path cells as that distance permits; the old one-cell cap no longer slows a person to a few metres per hour. Work rates remain model assumptions. Long-distance resupply and water travel remain unresolved; a route still needs enough provision-carrying capacity.

Ground distance uses the spherical projection, including latitude and longitude wrapping. A person's gathering choices use their dated local memories within a half-hour baseline walking radius; unseen sites need exploration. Camera distance does not determine access to resources.

An unhoused camp targets 16 m² per resident or the area required by its stock volume in 0.5 m open piles. Connected dry cells limit the achieved footprint; an island does not acquire new land to satisfy the target. Resting people and exposed-stock decay occupy these cells, and stock access follows the same connected footprint. This prevents all human and decomposer heat being concentrated at one coordinate. Piles, occupied volumes, hauling, local ventilation, and the thermal capacity of every stored material remain aggregate approximations.

Body wraps contain real fiber whose mass, density and conductivity determine thermal resistance. Maintenance, cold exposure and snow/ice melting require oxygen-limited oxidation of actual intake or usable body reserves; heat rejection consumes hydration. People carry up to 3 kg of rations independently of work cargo, remember locally observed water, and can interrupt work to reach it. These are reduced physical and behavioral mechanisms. They do not simulate core body temperature, organs, water containers or a complete thermoregulatory system.

Format 10 / biosphere-1.4 replaces automatic adult wrapping with performed body maintenance. An awake, sufficiently fed and hydrated adult can choose an existing repair task for themselves or another stationary person in the same 100 m² cell. Work moves accessible stock or carried fiber onto the body; removal returns usable fiber to an accessible stockpile or suitable cargo. Wear still transfers fiber into detritus. Placement is capped at 0.4 kg per work-hour and cannot run alongside resting or other work. Newly selected tasks begin earning protection on a later tick. Hunger, thirst, exhaustion, illness, sleep, movement and loss of contact can interrupt assistance.

The controller estimates thermal benefit from the same signed heat balance as physiology. Its 2 kg adult planning reference scales with represented body area; it is not a physical clothing limit, a calibrated infant garment or a recipe. Above skin temperature, insulation can reduce incoming heat; at equal temperature it supplies no sensible-heat benefit. A cell shares one local estimate per quarter-hour, with supplies/contact checked again at the transfer boundary. Choice probabilities, sociability, work rate, the age-12 work threshold and cell-level contact remain explicit approximations. Performed self-adjustment can redirect a helper's next choice; silence or a private task is not inferred consent. See the [body-maintenance release](releases/body-maintenance-0.2.md).

Body maintenance alone did not reconcile the baseline thermal estimate with respiration and body reserves. The format-12 correction below supplies that account. Successful local care or conserved metabolism still does not establish seasonal or generational survival.

Format 11 / biosphere-1.5 completes all current physiological withdrawals before optional personal ration refills, followed by decisions and movement. This prevents packing for later from preceding another person's meal, cold fuel or ice melting. Refill claims use one residual camp budget and one snapshot of contact before changing stock volume; movement, death, departure or changed custody invalidates a claim. Existing personal, cargo and caravan food is not silently redistributed. The 3 kg target, proportional contention and absence of handling labor are explicit controller approximations, not physical laws or freely developed institutions. Observers can inspect the actual personal-ration inventory. See the [ration-boundary release](releases/ration-boundary-0.2.md).

Format 12 / biosphere-1.6 introduces an unoxidized intake buffer and a usable reserve subset inside existing body material. Count body plus intake, never body plus reserves. One oxygen-limited oxidation pays for maintenance, signed thermal loss and finite ice melting; reserve use reduces both its tag and total body. Retention uses one processed-food allowance, restoring reserves before coarse structural growth. Current food and finite shared ice requests are resolved before optional meal buffers and personal rations. Work and journeys use their funded interval, and valid water-seeking routes persist through continuing thirst. See [funded metabolism](releases/funded-metabolism-0.2.md) for parameters, publication status and tests.

The `hunger`/`nourishment` signal now means intake fullness, with an explicit agent `satiety` alias. It is not a starvation clock or chemical-energy inventory. Empty intake can coexist with usable reserves; a high historical score cannot fund oxidation. New death records retain actual intake/reserve/body amounts and measured metabolic flux, while prior deaths remain unchanged. Initial reserves (10% of represented dry-equivalent body), release-power limits, retention and energy-deficit injury are uncalibrated assumptions. Oxygen contention, infant feeding, age-dependent growth and gestational energy remain unresolved.

Pulsed meals are tested through the separate intake and reserve compartments so that intervals between meals do not invent energy or automatically imply injury. Further work must establish feeding labor, age-dependent growth and physiological coefficients; neither historical philosophy nor the recorded infant's age establishes those coefficients.

The adult reserve heuristic uses current and earlier local daylight to anticipate the lean season, with an explicit food/spoilage estimate and at most 180 days of planned provisions. It does not grant food or consult future weather. Frozen soil stops photosynthesis but does not by itself imply drought damage to cold-tolerant dormant tissue. Respiration still consumes reserves; extreme cold, heat and unfrozen drought remain harmful.

Work selection uses a deterministic conditional sampler after urgent bodily and food needs. Each reached eligible branch receives its stated probability, including after a selected task proves unreachable; earlier choices cannot accidentally consume the entire numerical interval for later work. These are behavioral assumptions, not calibrated psychological laws. The planner tries ranked remembered alternatives through the shared construction validator, reserving material only for its first successful request. [The work-planning record](releases/work-planning-0.2.md) identifies the deployed runtime, its validation and the observed storage failure during activation.

Biological food loss depends on temperature, moisture and represented storage protection. The temperature factor applies to the whole loss rate; there is no additional temperature-independent biological floor. The current effective Q10 of 2 and positive cold-rate bound are assumptions, not a calibration for every food, humidity, packaging or microbial community. Spoiled material returns to the local detrital pools. Ancient storage observations and later measurements motivate this distinction; the [research record](research/2026-10-08-first-batch.md) separates those sources from parameter choices.

Format 13 / biosphere-1.7 applies the same exposure law to loose camp stocks, personal rations/work cargo and caravan provisions/goods. Exponential elapsed-hour loss replaces the separate daily camp charge. An hourly boundary uses each holder's current location and a full hour, even for goods acquired or moved shortly before it; exposure along individual transfers is not integrated. Actual camp storage retains its aggregate protection, while portable containers remain absent. Intake, body reserves and worn fiber keep their existing processes. Journey load now includes private food, covering and cargo within the existing 30 kg adult allowance; accepting return goods and taking raid goods use remaining living capacity. Deliveries credit actual surviving goods, so an exact-quantity dispatch can leave a small obligation requiring another journey. See [the release record](releases/food-custody-0.2.md) for validation and publication state.

The [scale research](research/README.md) and requirements P13–P16 in the [ledger](roadmap.md) organize further depth. A surface cell represents 100 m² in an equal-area projection; its two ground dimensions are not both 10 m away from the 45° standard parallel. Current loose-rock and mineral pools represent finite accessible material, not a full soil-to-core column. Buried trace inventories and geothermal forcing do not imply a resolved planetary interior. Depth, excavation, cavities, overburden, groundwater and underground construction require explicit representations and conservative transfers before they can affect inhabitants.

An assembly's name has no physical effect. Its potential cover, stability and capacity come from its material arrangement. Connected-volume integration rejects detached walls, side gaps and missing floors; a lower opening limits storage to its spill height. Autonomous search proposes general component operations without post, slab or named-building templates. Evidence matches geometry, measured properties and method: a contradictory trial remains distinct, and an idealized sample estimate cannot stand in for completed construction. Experiments still use small samples and idealized property estimates, selection uses fixed utility weights, and a bounded communal observation list can discard knowledge. These are explicit information-model limits, not finished scientific reasoning. Durable physical notes, uncertain predictions, material processing and tool-mediated operations remain prerequisites for broader invention; see the [construction review](research/open-construction.md).

Format 8 introduced cognitive/civic measurements at its intervention tick, preserving earlier observations and relationships as inherited accounts and honoring existing escrow. Format 9 added empty carried inventories to existing people and recalculated current structural affordances without changing material, identity or old observations. Format 10 changes future body work without transforming people, stocks, terrain, RNG or clock data; its new task fields are optional until work is chosen. Format 11 changes scheduling without changing prior inventories, tasks, identities or time. Format 12 labels a reserve subset inside the existing body and initializes empty intake without adding material or inferred historical meals. These migrations do not restore extinct populations. Any finite renewal has a separate authorized operator request, material boundary addition and permanent record. See [agency and diplomacy](agency-and-diplomacy.md) and [the implementation ledger](roadmap.md).

## Time and entropy

Time orders the changes. Entropy describes thermodynamic state and irreversible processes; elapsed time is not itself a quantity of entropy. Local biological complexity can increase because this is an open system receiving stellar energy and radiating heat.

The current ledger measures a **selected subset**, in kJ/K:

- Entropy produced by passive heat exchange between neighboring finite heat reservoirs, using their logarithmic temperature changes. Heat transfer cannot overshoot thermal equilibrium.
- An estimate of entropy delivered to surroundings by dissipated respiratory heat, `Q/T`.
- Absorbed stellar radiation, emitted surface thermal radiation, and atmospheric return radiation using the blackbody approximation `4E/(3T)`.

These counters are not total planetary or universal entropy. Full chemical reaction entropy, material mixing entropy, and many unresolved thermal reservoirs are absent. Radiation exchange is recorded as directional flows, separately from heat-mixing production. A migration begins a new measurement epoch at its actual tick; it does not invent measurements for the past.

The biochemical energy ledger is closed over the represented chemical pools and declared inputs/outputs. Energy and atmospheric accumulation retain numerical compensation across saves. Numerical tolerances follow the scale of processed quantities, not only the remaining living or stored pool. Significant violations still stop the simulation.

The planetary thermal model uses 36 equal-area bands, effective infrared emissivity 0.61, meridional diffusivity 0.6 W/m²/K, and effective thermal capacity 80,000 kJ/m²/K. These are model parameters representing unresolved atmosphere/ocean/ground behavior. Solar absorption, emission, band transport and local surface exchange have an explicit heat budget. The model begins at the intervention's actual tick from a stated climate prior, preserving existing tile temperatures. It does not invent earlier measurements, resolve all thermal reservoirs or establish a closed total-energy ledger for the entire planet.

## Initial conditions and fairness

The world starts old, vegetated, and inhabited by three human groups. It does not replay billions of years. New players begin remotely in unoccupied habitable terrain. The new founding baseline is 300 individually represented adults, with empty parent records denoting unrelated initial ancestry, a repeated 20–34-year age distribution, 150 initial pairs and independently permuted trait distributions. This is a selected migration group, not a complete historical population pyramid.

Each ordinary founder brings 48 kg food, 12 kg wood, 4 kg fiber, 2.25 kg stone and 0.75 kg clay; 2 kg of that fiber becomes a body wrap. Temperature/water/land screening also requires at least 20 kg local edible tissue and 10 kg local wood per founder on represented land connected within a 480 m projected radius. Paths detouring outside that radius are not counted. Equal initial terms do not guarantee equivalent weather, affordable harvest, ecological replacement, exclusive catchments or survival. The screen does not give people personal knowledge of those resource locations. The separate restoration intervention has larger finite reserves to repair the damaged historical world; ordinary founders do not receive repeated restoration grants.

The [historical trials](validation/founding-trials.json) compared four, eight and twelve adults over fourteen days. They are not evidence for the new 300-person baseline or a biologically established viable population. The current trial program accepts explicit population sizes and retains old reports rather than rewriting past evidence.

The [actual finite renewal and follow-up](validation/community-renewal-2026-10-08.md) restored four historical groups at tick 195860 and retained all 1,200 through the independently verified tick-204116 backup. Housing remains far below population and the reserves are finite. Failed coupled seasonal trials remain part of the evidence; the restoration is not proof of long-term ecosystem or demographic equilibrium.

The later [collapse investigation](research/community-collapse-2026-10-08.md) records all 1,200 renewed adults and their 407 children dying by tick 248627. A historical replay proves the first infant's final health loss came from cold exposure, with stock fiber available but no caregiver wrapping path. Controlled checks reproduce update-order allocation of scarce communal food. Child growth, body-reserve metabolism, shelter, storage loss and connected subsistence capacity remain uncalibrated. These failures invalidate a claim of natural or generationally viable balance; they are not repaired by the later work-planning hotfix alone.

Those trials describe biosphere-1.1. The live record subsequently shows all three original groups dying by tick 7712, about 80 simulated days. The later saved checkpoint at tick 20244 contains no surviving people or animal cohorts, with 132 small plant cohorts remaining. The [recovery investigation](validation/recovery-2026-10-08.md) records the overheating, numerical halt, candidate failures and corrective mechanisms. These findings rule out treating the opening trials as lasting balance validation. Deaths and prior history remain preserved.

Conservation includes newly materialized terrain and founders as explicit boundary arrivals. Nothing in an agent's action can directly mint elemental mass or give a community an invented building bonus.

## Subsequent interventions

Further development should extend mechanisms while preserving the already living world. Priorities are:

1. Validate seasonal and multi-generation ecosystem/demographic behavior, including extinction, migration, reproduction, and carrying capacity.
2. Introduce a conservative hierarchy of planetary and regional transport so distant active ecosystems can scale without simulating every cell at the same detail.
3. Replace bulk approximations with useful reaction, material-processing, hydrology, disease, and inheritance mechanisms where they materially expand emergence.
4. Expand agent observations, local institutions, memory, and collaborative behavior without privileged recipe unlocks.
5. Add recoverable player accounts and operational monitoring before broad public growth.

These are engineering changes to the simulation, not a technology progression imposed on its inhabitants. Every change to a live law or saved representation needs a migration/continuity argument, validation, and a recorded intervention.

The [fundamentals review](fundamentals.md) expands the unresolved foundations, including continuous distant ecosystems, open-ended material interactions, local information, demographic viability, and external-agent decision time.

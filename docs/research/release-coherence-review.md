This is a concrete information inconsistency, not a claim that the sample physically measured a full-sized container. The original result is retained in the record.

The corrected `matchingObservation` reuses a note only when **geometry, research method and physical result agree**. Contradictory retests and construction following a sample create separate evidence. An agreeing repeat reused that current note; a method-control sample preserved a previous construction note. The parent reports **11/11** knowledge-record and existing depth-suite regressions passing; research also inspected the construction-completion regression, without rerunning that suite.

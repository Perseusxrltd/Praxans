# Food, custody and sustainable access

This follow-up to the [collapse investigation](community-collapse-2026-10-08.md) traces food through the current simulation. The historical replay proves the first infant's thermal death and controlled cases prove ration-allocation defects. The additional defects below are source findings, not an attribution of the 1,607 renewed deaths.

The prior readings of Xenophon's *Oeconomicus* IX/XVI and the *Arthashastra* II.15 motivate questions about stores, replenishment, losses and local growing conditions. They do not supply physiological coefficients or prescribe the communities' institutions. Exact editions, passages and limits remain in the [source register](source-register.json), entries A04 and A08. No new historical reading or empirical calibration is claimed by this batch.

## Three reproducible inconsistencies

| Boundary | Earlier behavior | Correction and remaining limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loose material exposure | Camp stocks decayed; personal food, work cargo and journey stores were exempt merely because of their custody. | The same elapsed-hour exposure law acts at each actual location. Existing camp storage supplies its represented protection. Swallowed intake, body material and worn fiber retain their own physiological/wear processes. No food-specific microbes or portable packaging are represented. |
| Journey load | The 30 kg adult allowance counted shared journey food and goods but omitted personal rations and actual covering. | Formation, carrier loss, exchange and raid capacity include each living carrier's external material. The fixed allowance and three-person limit remain controller assumptions. |
| Founding resources | A radial survey counted edible tissue and wood across impassable water. | Only represented land connected within that radius supplies the screen. This does not grant knowledge, title, harvest labor, exclusive catchments or sustainable annual production. |

The corrections and validation are tracked in the [release record](../releases/food-custody-0.2.md). Eliminating an exemption from spoilage can reduce available food; it must not be described as a survival boost. Existing sites do not relocate when the founding screen changes.

The bounded independent source review found two integration qualifications. Exposure is sampled hourly at the current location, including a full-hour charge for goods acquired or moved shortly before that boundary; the first post-migration boundary has the same approximation. A successful fixed-environment subdivision test does not establish exact transport exposure. Delivery accords credit actual received material, so dispatching exactly the outstanding quantity can require another trip after spoilage. The controller permits fractional remainder shipments and uses a 1e-8 kg fulfillment tolerance; these are explicit conventions, not extra material or proof of realistic logistics.

## The food account to measure next

The runtime records metabolic intervals, but it does **not** yet persist a complete daily food-flow account. A [subsequent bounded measurement](food-flux-day-2026-10-09.md) now separates these flows on an inhabited copy with exact baseline/observer replay agreement. It is a single current-law day, not historical attribution or annual balance. A useful account must distinguish physical transformations from changes of holder:

| Flow | Source and destination | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Edible production and harvest | Local plants/forage to personal work cargo | Photosynthesis, edible allocation and extraction are separate rates. Animals also compete for local tissue. Human hunting/fishing does not currently supply a parallel food path. |
| Delivery and packing | Work cargo, communal stock, personal rations, journey goods | Transfers between holders; summing them as fresh production would count the same material repeatedly. |
| Ingestion | Accessible outside-body food to actual intake | A custody change into the body, not oxidation and not additional reserves. |
| Oxidation | Intake or reserve subset to respiration products and heat | Count each substrate once. Reserve oxidation also reduces total body; maintenance, cold and ice melting cannot each burn that same packet again. |
| Retention | Intake to total body, sometimes tagged as reserves | Reserve material is included in body. Structural growth and reserve recovery share the processed-food allowance. |
| Spoilage and death | Actual loose goods, intake or body to local reservoirs | Keep food loss separate from mortality. A dead person's remaining material returns once at their location. |
| Birth, trade, movement and arrival | Specific local holders, parent body, foreign community or world boundary | A world-wide ledger cancels internal transfers. Community accounts retain both sides. New terrain/founders and explicit operator arrivals are external boundaries. |

A proposed diagnostic should keep bounded counters per community/day and quantity, not a permanent event for every meal. Capture the completed metabolic interval after optional ingestion has updated it and before death removes the person; finalize daily values after that day's births, movements, journeys and losses. Record opening and closing quantities, source, destination, cause, kilograms and the measurement's starting tick. Do not fill earlier days with invented measurements or reveal private knowledge to inhabitants.

A useful survival trial then separates standing edible tissue, connected habitat, personally remembered patches, completed routes, extracted kg/day, delivered kg/day and ecological replacement. The historical three-camp catchment overlap remains unresolved. A founding threshold is not a carrying-capacity calculation.

## Other open physical boundaries

Birth currently uses the home stock/water and creates the child at home even if the parent is elsewhere. Local gestation, birth, feeding and material access require their own correction. The newborn begins with **1 kg hydration**; the existing one-kilogram resource guard funds that initial quantity. The larger hydration target is a desired later fill, not evidence that birth creates unfunded water.

Branch stock relocation and social provisioning still use coarse transfers. Portable containment, handling labor, child feeding, age-dependent growth, food quality, seasonal replacement and multiple communities competing for the same catchment need explicit mechanisms and discriminating tests. These are recorded questions, not implemented systems or validated natural balance.

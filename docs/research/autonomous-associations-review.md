# Associations initiated by inhabitants — 8 October 2026

**Start with a resident asking a nearby person for help on work they have actually witnessed.** Delivered requests, expressed assent, contributions and renewed commitments can produce a continuing association. A later delegation can authorize a known participant to coordinate a limited part of the others' offered work. Neither action needs an external agent, a government name or a population threshold.

This is a proposed extension to the [membership/authority review](open-social-organization-review.md), supporting [H06](../../docs/roadmap.md). Research inspected source and the existing source register only; no simulation, database, host or Git operation was performed.

## Questions from the existing sources

[Ibn Khaldun's first prefatory discussion](https://www.muslimphilosophy.com/ik/Muqaddimah/Chapter1/Ch_1_01.htm), source A09, asks how complementary work and tools enable provisioning. [Polybius VI.9, 11–18](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Polybius/6*.html), A07, relates authority to other people's decisions and supplies. These suggest examining practical dependence and recognized powers. Their necessary royal authority, constitutional preferences and alleged cycles are historical interpretations, not model laws. The [register](../../docs/research/source-register.json) records the passages consulted on 8 October 2026; this review adds no historical measurements. The generator, preferences and numerical limits below are engineering hypotheses, not empirically calibrated anthropology.

## What the current source actually provides

- [cognition.ts](../../src/simulation/cognition.ts), `updateMind` 102–109: stationary `social` activity reduces social need even without another person. [citizens.ts](../../src/simulation/citizens.ts), `finishTask` 479–488, grants happiness/energy and can record shared stories whether or not `teachNearby` finds a listener. A task label therefore cannot serve as evidence of a relationship.
- `teachNearby`, cognition 269–300, selects the first eligible same-civilization listener within 1.5 coordinate cells. It checks attention and sleeping, but has no explicit listener assent or listener time allocation. Its global array search and fixed teaching-value ranking should not become a political contact/leadership selector.
- `Mind` in [types.ts](../../src/simulation/types.ts), 212–237, has activity learning, place memories and observation traces, but no counterpart-specific work history or promises. Current limits are 48 places and 32 knowledge traces. `reinforce` changes an activity preference, not trust in a named coworker.
- Citizens 725–795 sums actual assembly work, but only the person whose increment finishes the structure receives the construction observation and reinforcement. Other contributors subsequently clear their completed task. A completion event does not identify all helpers or justify selecting the final worker as leader.
- [society.ts](../../src/simulation/society.ts), `autonomousDiplomacy` 342–401, generates inhabitant proposals to answer delivered diplomatic terms. It does not originate local associations. The [community planner](../../src/simulation/engine.ts), 167–211, and citizens' project lookup at 256–258 use community-wide information; they are not evidence that a particular resident knows a job or another person's capacity.

## Smallest useful generator

**First target: an existing, funded, unfinished local assembly.** Its geometry remains arbitrary and its material already has a ledger. Add coordination of actual work before adding group ownership or a new communal treasury. The same request mechanism can later address other represented activities.

1. **Notice a specific difficulty.** At a work pause or conversation, a person uses their dated observation of the job and their own recent work rate to estimate whether desired remaining work exceeds the time they currently expect to offer. This estimate can be wrong. If help appears worthwhile after communication/travel costs, they may request it from someone actually encountered. Do not search the world's best worker, current private needs or distant projects. Preserve urgent food, water and sleep decisions.
2. **Exchange a concrete offer.** Communicate job ID/location, proposed interval, requested effort, the requester's offered effort and expiry. Both people spend encounter time. A recipient may accept, decline or offer a smaller/different interval based on their own needs, preferences, known obligations and experience with this counterpart. Silence is not assent. Acceptance creates an immutable, dated shared commitment with individual receipts; a later revision needs another exchange. Helping once does not imply membership in every group the requester knows.
3. **Do ordinary work.** An accepted promise supplies a candidate to the person's decision path; it cannot write another resident's task or defeat bodily interruption. Each person still performs at most one task at a time. Record promised/observed person-hours separately from the engine's work units. Accumulate actual contributions to the job without a cooperation multiplier. Travel and conversation consume time that cannot simultaneously count as construction. If circumstances change, retain the unmet promise and any received explanation rather than manufacturing its completion.
4. **Remember specific evidence.** Each participant learns their own contribution and only the coworker attendance/contribution they witnessed or were told about. Keep source, date and uncertainty. A refusal supplies information about availability or preferences; it is not automatically dishonesty. An interruption observed during illness differs from an unexplained absence. Neither improves or damages every resident's trust through a community-wide success score. Aggregate contributions within an active commitment rather than recording every tick as a new permanent event.
5. **Renew or end an arrangement.** When another locally known need arises, residents may reuse a familiar partner, negotiate different terms, or work independently. Only an acknowledged continuing scope constitutes standing membership. Assign its group ID without converting the connected component of the social graph into one organization. A person may agree separately with two overlapping groups; leaving one does not end the other or erase earlier obligations.
6. **Offer limited coordination.** Repeated direct negotiation can reveal a cost: time spent contacting people or resolving conflicting work windows. A resident may then offer a known coworker authority to choose among explicitly agreed jobs/windows within that resident's remaining promised hours. The proposed coordinator must also accept and have time to communicate. Start from witnessed work reliability, not a pre-existing leadership rank, so delegation can arise without seeding an office. Orders still require delivery, stay within scope and can be refused or revoked. A delegated ceiling and its suballocations share one capacity budget.

Steps 1, 2, 5 and 6 need explicit personal choice policies. Initially use existing bodily signals and activity dispositions plus locally remembered costs/outcomes, and publish the added feature weights as assumptions. Do not use the assembly's common utility table as everyone's political preference. Learning may alter predictions; it does not make a fixed reward function a theory of human motives.

## Finite bounds and non-emergence

A small experimental configuration could allow one pending request, four active commitments and 16 recent contacts per person, with one mutually attended exchange occupying one quarter-hour tick. These are chosen computational/protocol bounds, not measured human capacities or universal conversation duration. Account for both participants' time and prevent an actor from attending several exchanges in the same tick. A full active-commitment budget defers new requests; it must not silently delete old promises. Forgotten personal evidence and an archived commitment are distinct records.

Use a shared local encounter lookup and bounded candidate rotation; avoid always selecting the first citizen in array order. The current 1.5-cell teaching distance is a compatibility reference, not a calibrated number of metres. Reuse the world's clock and declare the encounter distance contract. Relationships identify people, not a required common `civId`; cross-community comprehension/access remains an explicit later integration requirement.

Recurrent interdependence, useful information and willingly renewed arrangements may support enduring authority. Isolation, sufficient solo capacity, refusal, incompatible purposes or excessive coordination cost may leave only temporary cooperation—or none. No number of successes automatically creates a council, permanent leader or government. Current work is mostly additive, so this pilot cannot establish that hierarchy improves productivity. Coercion, contested possession and succession remain separately modeled possibilities with physical consequences, as in the preceding review.

## Small causal acceptance cases

| Fixture                                                                                                                             | Required observation                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Three residents, a witnessed funded job, suitable personal choices; no agent submission, group or mandate preloaded                 | A generated request is received, separately accepted and followed by actual work. Capture the full notice → offer → receipt → contribution chain over a few decision/encounter steps.                            |
| Same fixture with no nearby listener, then with a listener who declines or has urgent thirst                                        | No fabricated encounter, assent, membership or contribution. A solitary social task cannot create peer trust or a shared-story record.                                                                           |
| Two participants in two overlapping arrangements; overlapping work windows                                                          | Counteroffer/refusal or an explicitly unmet promise; never duplicate labor, inventory or physiology. Check contribution equals ordinary executed work after travel/conversation time.                            |
| Several actual contributors; change which person happens to finish the assembly                                                     | Recorded witnessed contributions remain attributable to the correct people. Finishing last grants no automatic leadership or group-wide knowledge.                                                               |
| Repeated costly negotiations, then an offered narrow delegation; otherwise identical fixture with refusal or cheap independent work | Authority follows delivered assent only in the first case. Test out-of-scope orders, exhausted hours and delivered revocation without undoing earlier work. Different or absent organizations are valid results. |

These are proposed tests, not results. Save/reload must preserve IDs, receipts, deadlines and outstanding commitments under the explicitly required **format-10 migration**. Keep existing civilian affiliation, stocks, assemblies and accords intact. The prior review's measured migration-headroom gate still applies. Implement one local work/encounter path first; do not combine it with a full political redesign or claim that adding group records has solved autonomous institutions.

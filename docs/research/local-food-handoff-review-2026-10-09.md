# Local food handoff: implementation review

Reviewed the pre-handoff source reported at `46be825`, format 13 / `biosphere-1.7`. This is a design review and proposed acceptance set, not a passing implementation report. The [infant diagnostic](infant-food-access-2026-10-09.md) showed that nearby adult-held food remained inaccessible while infant-held food entered intake. Reserve use caused no injury during those four hours. A voluntary transfer can address that represented access gap; it cannot establish realistic feeding or long-term survival.

## Integration conditions

[bodywork.ts](../../src/simulation/bodywork.ts), `beginBodyWork`, `contact`, `workOnBody` and `finishBodyWork`, provides local contact, earned work and a shared transfer boundary. Reuse those constraints without copying fiber-specific rewards or declaring the wrapping rate an empirical food-handling rate.

Three points need explicit treatment:

- **Phase:** [citizens.ts](../../src/simulation/citizens.ts), `updateCitizens`, creates the contact snapshot before metabolism, meals and ration packing. Food budgets and recipient inventory gaps need a common post-meal state before any handoff credit. Cap against actual remaining stock; debit every source before crediting any recipient. Delivered food becomes available for the next physiological interval.
- **Dispatch:** `updateCitizenActivity` currently recognizes `isBodyRepair` for paid work, hunger interruption and nighttime continuation. A food task must enter the intended personal-work path, not fall through generic structure repair. Selecting it earns zero work; existing task identity and funded activity limit its next interval.
- **Persistence:** [engine.ts](../../src/simulation/engine.ts), `validateWorld`, currently permits recipient-bearing repair tasks only for fiber/wrap targets. New task meaning needs unambiguous validation and explicit saved-format/law migration. Preserve partial wrap work, inventories, histories and stale-recipient cancellation; reject mixed wrap/food/structure targets.

The first transfer should use the donor's actual outside-body food. Intake and reserve/body matter are different compartments. Community membership alone must not authorize withdrawing distant camp or caravan supplies. Donor self-preservation belongs to its decision controller, not a universal prohibition on sacrifice. Nearby need estimates must be distinguished from exact private recipient intake/reserve knowledge. No kinship, common civilization, assembly or delegation requirement is physically necessary.

## Limits and interpretation

A handoff places food in represented custody. It is not lactation, preparation, swallowing assistance or consent to ingestion. Handling rates and inventory targets remain explicit controller approximations. A sleeping infant's lack of response is unknown, not agreement; capable recipients' withdrawal can affect contact. If explicit acceptance, rejection or disposal is absent, document that approximation. Do not award fictional nutritional success for a delivery; distinguish observed delivery from later ingestion and paid handling practice from free reward on assignment.

The current automatic ingestion and heat-linked retention rule remain separate abstractions. The diagnostic's increased retention under additional funded cold demand was an uncalibrated response, not biological validation or a measured historical causal share.

Previously consulted Xenophon, _Oeconomicus_ IX (A04), frames availability and use of stores; it does not prescribe household roles. FAO/WHO/UNU energy chapters (M16–M17) distinguish expenditure and retained tissue. Their [registered access scope](source-register.json) remains unchanged; no source supplies this handoff rate, willingness rule or infant feeding coefficients. No new external reading or simulation was needed for this review.

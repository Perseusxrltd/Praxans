# Local membership and scoped authority — 8 October 2026

**The first increment should be a locally recognized, revocable mandate over specific work or existing resources, with membership independent of civilization affiliation.** Keep the present assembly as an existing institution while participants form overlapping associations and exercise limited delegated powers. Creating an association should not require a community vote; spending that community's stores still requires authority over those stores. This is a proposed mechanism, not implemented political freedom or a claim that voluntary agreement explains every historical institution.

This read-only review supports [H06](../../docs/roadmap.md) and the [accepted political agency contract](../../docs/agency-and-diplomacy.md). Research made no source changes, Git operations, live requests, database reads or simulation runs. The [sampler review](conditional-labor-sampler-review.md) remains separate.

## A bounded first mechanism

1. **Participants and acknowledgments.** Give associations stable IDs and descriptive names, without a government-type field. Store membership assertions, each participant's acknowledgment or dissent, and when/where that knowledge arrived. A claimed member need not be a consenting member. Keep `civId` for existing provisioning and census behavior initially. Joining an association changes neither bodily location nor ownership.
2. **A mandate with a real subject.** Record issuer, holder, the controlled stock/asset or willing person's work, permitted operations, quantitative ceiling, expiry tick, delegation scope and recognition receipts. A name or title provides no authority by itself. Begin with one finite material/work allocation from an existing accepted assembly; participants may also agree about their own work without assembly approval. Residents retain bodily priorities and can refuse a task. External agents continue to propose through recognized authority rather than directly assigning tasks or spending stock.
3. **Delegation without multiplying power.** A child mandate must narrow its parent's operations, resources, quantity and duration. All descendants consume the same underlying allowance; a parent and child cannot each spend its full nominal budget. One-level delegation is a reasonable initial engineering bound, explicitly not a political law. Keep free prose descriptive and execute only supported physical operations; do not add constitution presets or code execution through a charter.
4. **Custody and conserved transfers.** Authorization is neither material nor a second stock. Initially, cite the existing communal stock and its actual camp-access check as a coarse custody approximation. Recheck available goods when acted upon. If material is reserved, count it once and expose which existing obligations can already consume it. A request must never debit communal and group accounts independently or create a fictional local store. Preserve the 1,600 kg assembly bound and existing construction/work rules.
5. **Receipt-dependent revocation.** Ending a mandate and delivering that news are separate events. A custodian denies later use after receiving revocation; a distant actor may still hold stale information. Record disputed intervening acts rather than silently reversing transfers. Delegating descendants need notices too. Pre-agreed expiry can use the common simulation tick; do not use wall time. This concerns in-world knowledge, not server credential revocation.
6. **Obligations survive changes of organization.** Refusing membership or a task differs from defaulting on an accepted promise. Withdrawal, dissent, a holder's death or a new group name does not erase that promise, release in-transit cargo or appoint an heir. Keep the original obligor, custodian and receipts until a recognized transfer, fulfillment or recorded failure. Splitting affiliations cannot teleport people or goods. A rejected authorization also does not make unauthorized taking physically impossible: future seizure/coercion must involve actual access, force, resistance and costs. This first cooperation mechanism does not yet model those processes, and neither a leader's title nor an aggregate score may force compliance.

Trust should next become a sparse relationship with a known counterparty and domain, revised from witnessed or delivered outcomes. Retain the old scalar as a legacy general expectation during transition, without inventing an individual adviser history from it. The present proposal has only an `agentName`; recognized persistent principals need explicit identity provenance. No universal success score should automatically confer legitimacy or force obedience.

## Small acceptance cases

- **Overlap and refusal:** eight existing people, groups `{A,B,C,D}` and `{C,D,E,F}`, two unaffiliated. One person declines a request. All eight receive exactly one physiological update; C/D cannot perform two simultaneous tasks or cast extra votes in one decision merely by overlapping memberships. Membership creates no citizens, goods or access rights.
- **Shared ceiling:** from 50 kg of existing wood, authorize at most 20 kg; delegate an 8 kg sublimit. A direct 12 kg transfer and the delegated 8 kg transfer exhaust the same allowance, leaving 30 kg in the source and 20 kg in actual destinations/cargo. Duplicate requests and additional spending fail without a second debit. If transport/work is needed, authorization alone cannot complete it.
- **Delayed notice:** send revocation while the holder/custodian is distant; stop observation before and after delivery. Verify unchanged distant knowledge before receipt, refusal after receipt, retained earlier transfers and a single chronology. Losing a messenger must delay knowledge rather than cancel the underlying history.
- **Death and split:** withdraw a member or remove a holder through an existing death event while a delivery is pending. The old obligation/cargo IDs remain valid; no automatic successor receives powers; outstanding resources are neither copied nor lost. A small association can split without a 300-person founding operation.
- **Different outcomes from relationships:** with equal supplies and no government preset, one fixture acknowledges a delegate and another refuses or divides the same proposed scope. Execution follows these different receipts. A title, better aggregate score or an adviser request cannot override the second fixture's refusal. Then save/reload and continue each chronology with the same IDs and logical clock.

These are proposed causal fixtures, not completed tests or predictions about durable political forms.

## Schema continuity and delivery boundary

The parent requires an explicit **format-10 migration** for saved membership/mandate/receipt records; do not add them silently to format 9. Retain existing `civics`, pending ballots, accords, caravan references, claims, people and stocks. An adapter can address the current assembly through its existing civilization ID. Do not fabricate past acknowledgments or rewrite old proposals to use new membership rules. Membership withdrawal can end future participation while an institution remains addressable for outstanding obligations. Migration must preserve the exact tick, RNG and physical reservoirs; later new behavior need not reproduce the old engine's trajectory.

Implement recognition and authorization in simulation logic. Server authentication bounds what an external client can propose; it must not manufacture resident assent. The executor needs an explicit actor, resource source and mandate context, rather than a copied civilization object with altered stock. Start with one local association and one real supported operation. Until that operation debits the proper source and lets participants refuse, extra group fields are only descriptive metadata.

Index active records by participant, resource and due tick; process local encounters/deliveries and scheduled deadlines rather than all person/group pairs every tick. Retain active obligations and supporting receipts through UI-cache eviction; expose bounded observer summaries. Actual writing/storage carriers and indefinite history retention remain separate capacity work.

**Activation gate:** the parent's 8 October capacity report records a 1 GB memory/500 MB nominal-volume host, a roughly 253 MB database and an observed 109 MB active WAL. These are parent-supplied observations, not research host reads; subtraction from nominal volume does not establish usable migration headroom. Before activation, measure disposable migration peak memory, disk/WAL growth, elapsed time, archive continuity and reload behavior with outstanding proposals/deliveries. Preserve an independently verified backup outside the constrained live volume. A checkpointed retained-WAL limit does not bound active WAL growth. The political schema must wait for measured headroom, even if its empty-record migration appears small.

This is subsequent mechanism work, not a requirement to bundle a political redesign into the narrow construction/labor correction. Until these cases pass, describe the existing assembly accurately and keep H06 unresolved.

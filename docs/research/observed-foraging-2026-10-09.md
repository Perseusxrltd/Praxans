# Knowing a patch is empty — 9 October 2026

**People could keep gathering after personally observing that the source was empty.** Their remembered observation changed, but an ongoing resource task did not reconsider it. The controller now allows a strictly later observation to end that attempt. In a matched inhabited-copy day, work under that known contradiction falls to zero and total harvest rises 1.73%. Individual outcomes remain uneven: Amber Hollow gathers less, aggregate infant cold injury rises, and harvesting still falls below oxidation. This is a correction to information use, not demonstrated survival improvement.

The [quantities and provenance](observed-foraging-2026-10-09.json) retain both source fingerprints, full final world digests, work closure and physiological outcomes. The [release record](../releases/observed-foraging-0.2.md) distinguishes validation from publication. The [historical collapse study](community-collapse-2026-10-08.md) remains the source for demonstrated historical causes; this current-law counterfactual assigns no additional death share.

## What changes a decision

The relevant information is a person's own dated observation at the task's destination, made after the matching recorded experience began. It can concern food, wood, fiber, stone or clay. The controller reads coordinates and that memory; it does not query hidden current stock or another worker's intentions. An earlier or same-tick empty observation does not prohibit a new attempt. Positive small quantities remain physically collectable.

Physiology still funds the interval before reconsideration. Cancellation creates no food, returns no spent effort and performs no replacement action in that interval. It preserves actual carried matter and any separately existing construction. Saved tasks and memories load exactly; ordinary subsequent behavior applies the new controller. There is no new saved field, physical extraction equation or migration.

Six causal cases check the fresh/stale distinction, small positive yield, stone exhaustion, old and same-interval observations, unseen distant changes and save/load continuity. The initial code fails the new-observation and saved-task controls. A first candidate using an inclusive timestamp also failed the subsequently added same-interval control; the final rule requires a strictly later tick. All six final controls and the full 245-case model/server suite pass.

## One inhabited day

The same preserved checkpoint at tick 228436 contains 1,200 adults and 239 children under one. Both arms run 96 ticks, or 24 hours, under format 15 / biosphere-1.9. They end with 1,441 people after two ordinary births and no new deaths. Each read-only observed execution matches its uninstrumented reference's complete final world digest. This is not a historical-law replay: inherited oversized children and initially empty migrated intake buffers make it an unsuitable steady-state food budget.

Before the correction, 494 gathering intervals proceed under a strictly later personal empty observation. Of 1,578 zero-yield completions, 97 have that information at completion; the other 1,481 do not. Some sources are physically empty while memory still reports food. This separates known contradiction from unobserved depletion and competition. The categories of current empty memory and an earlier empty-work interval overlap; they must not be added as independent causes.

After the correction, 220 gathering cancellations have the exhaustion condition. Work at physically empty sources falls from 1,829.591 to 1,649.466 effective hours, but remains substantial. These changes include subsequent choices and divergent RNG trajectories; they do not isolate a recoverable food quantity for every abandoned hour.

Cancelled gathering progress rises from 307.297 to 523.334 effective hours. Some accumulated progress predates the checkpoint. The present batch convention has not yet made a product from that effort, so cancellation destroys no material and must not issue compensatory food. This exposes a separate modeling boundary: [performed harvesting and simultaneous source claims](harvesting-work-design-2026-10-09.md) need their own physical design and causal controls. That design is not implemented in this release.

## Adverse outcomes and remaining causes

Food oxidation is 1,134.969 kg before and 1,133.667 kg after, greater than harvested food in both arms even before spoilage. Children’s cold shortfall increases from 18,091.156 to 19,870.180 kJ; summed metabolic health-point loss rises from 75.380 to 82.792 across the measured intervals. Those sums exclude recovery and other health changes and are not final health differences. Lowest observed child health increases from 93.855 to 94.076, illustrating why neither one aggregate nor one extreme establishes overall welfare. Adult metabolic health loss remains zero in this window.

The correction is justified by the reproduced failure to use newly acquired information, not by a promise that every better-informed choice wins. It does not repair competing construction reservations, learned valuation of particular food sites, observation while travelling, partial work products, developmental handling or seasonal ecological replacement. The gathering reward network still does not select the present gathering branches. These remain explicit controller and physical-model limitations, not natural laws.

Gathering progress closes within 6.8×10⁻¹² effective hours and whole-world relative elemental residual stays below 1.86×10⁻¹⁰. Those checks establish the stated accounts, not a closed total-energy ledger. Local elapsed time and memory measurements under differing concurrent work are not a performance comparison or proof of inhabited operation within the live host's 1 GB limit.

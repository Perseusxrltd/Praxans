A large live read-only audit triggered an OOM restart; retaining a compressed backup exhausted volume space until cleanup/recovery. These are parent-supplied operational observations, not independently repeated experiments. “Read-only” describes authority, not memory or disk cost.

## First boundary: local interactions and bounded publication

1. **Index current entities before changing their laws.** Disease exposure in [citizens.ts](../../src/simulation/citizens.ts) scans the population per person hourly. Shelter occupancy, teaching, family pairing and civic preferences also repeat global/local-population searches. `detectContacts` in [diplomacy.ts](../../src/simulation/diplomacy.ts) enumerates awake pairs: up to **719,400** at 1,200 people, **71,994,000** at 12,000, and **7,199,940,000** at 120,000. These are loop bounds, not measured timings. Index people/structures by location and ID; preserve current candidate order, tie-breaking and RNG consumption in the initial optimization.
2. **Separate global summaries, local visual detail and selected records.** Publish immutable envelopes with world ID, tick, law/generation/projection version, bounds, entity IDs, revisions and delta base. Global population/region totals and bounded or clustered community markers must not embed citizens or construction records. A local window selects spatial entities, including travelers; an inspector requests paginated rich records by stable ID. Agent views need their own local-knowledge filter, not spectator omniscience.
3. **Bound work and bytes together.** Share global accounting once per publication, then add local environment data. Use capped pages, deltas, cancellation, revision caches and total outgoing-buffer limits. The existing 100-stream cap and 4 MiB per-stream backpressure check do not bound aggregate strings/copies or a single oversized write. Losing observer detail may trigger resynchronization; it must never change the world clock or remove inhabitants.
4. **Budget operation overlap.** Validate hotfixes/audits off the live host where possible, retaining only bounded metadata checks locally. Account for database, WAL, migration archives, backup output and temporary copies together. A compressed backup beside its source still consumes space. Capacity checks and retention must preserve a verified independent recovery copy; deleting history is not a scaling mechanism. Stream or page audits/checkpoints rather than materializing every JSON row at once.

The server's pump yields after roughly 50 ms **between** complete ticks. It cannot preempt a single expensive tick. Meeting tick-throughput and health latency therefore requires bounded inner work, not only additional `setImmediate` calls around it.

## Regional simulation without disconnected worlds

Keep a region as a **spatial owner of state**, not a civilization. A large civilization may span regions; different civilizations share water, pathogens, land and trade routes. Start with one coordinator and indexed region work queues. Only then introduce workers that calculate proposed transfers against an identified epoch; the coordinator validates and commits each transaction once. Do not create independently ticking copies of the SQLite world.

Every region needs bounds/area/depth scope, last integrated tick, pool owners and revisions. Cross-boundary movement, water, heat, seed dispersal and communication need explicit source, destination, amount and time. Read-only neighbor copies own no additional stock. A full-rest adult can walk **450 ground metres per quarter-hour tick**, exceeding a **320 m projected region side** near the original latitude. Halos or event exchanges must follow each process's actual reach and crossing time, not assume one adjacent cell is enough. Shared atmospheric and climatic reservoirs need one owner and conservative exchanges.

`materializeChunk` currently introduces declared boundary inventories and computes global before/after element ledgers. It does not refine an already inventoried coarse ecosystem. `stepWorld` continues detailed updates for all materialized land; no unload/reload ecology exists. A future coarse region must advance physically relevant state while unobserved and expose unresolved uncertainty. Do not freeze drought, aging, stock loss or commitments until a camera returns. Shared RNG and update order currently couple regions: thread-safe replay requires an explicit versioned random-stream/event-order contract, not merely matching seeds.

## Coarse and fine state: compression must preserve lives and matter

First compact storage and skip provably irrelevant work while retaining individual IDs. All current humans are named individuals; replacing them with anonymous counts would lose relationships, history and knowledge. A future coarse human representation needs persistent identity/kinship/record holders plus sufficient age, health, skill and location distributions. If that information cannot be preserved or bounded in error, retain individual records and change the execution/storage architecture.

Promotion to finer detail must **partition**, never add, population, body matter, hydration, provisions, cargo, stock, biomass, minerals and chemical energy. Coarsening recombines those totals. Temperature and averages are not conserved inventories; transfer heat/enthalpy with the declared capacity. Births, deaths and migration remain distinct integer events. No new people, food, tools, ancestry or knowledge appear because a view zooms in. Nonlinear hunger, contagion and scarcity require distributions or explicit individuals; one average can conceal deaths.

Refine because a represented interaction or error bound requires it, not because someone is watching. Use small fine-versus-coarse comparisons under a cold period, food depletion and cross-boundary migration, then test repeated refine/coarsen cycles. Preserve the failed combined 32-founder seasonal evidence: separate regional success did not establish coupled-world success. Depth, excavation and underground support remain [P14–P16 requirements](../roadmap.md), not implicit capabilities of surface chunks.

## Open creations need operations and carriers, not larger menus

Current arbitrary construction means five bulk media and at most 32 axis-aligned cuboids, with finite samples/work and connected containment. The 1,600 kg request cap, 8 m height, automatic nearby sites, absent processing/joints/tools, and fixed utility thresholds still exclude many useful constructions. Simply raising the component cap makes the exact face partition grow roughly cubically. Extend local physical interactions and reusable spatial structure first; let a larger creation span supported parts/sites with actual force, material, access and work paths. Recipes record the operations and evidence **after** discovery. Neither names nor agent-written descriptions grant effects.

Large institutions also exceed the present representation: one civilization has one camp/stock footprint and a local assembly within three projected cells, while quorum is at least **50% of all adults**. Constitution changes still use that same gate. Enforceable diplomatic terms are limited to peace, passage and transfer; free text is correctly inert. These are chosen social/protocol bounds, not natural laws or general institutional emergence.

The next bounded institutional mechanism is a physically communicated, revocable authorization/commitment between identified holders, scoped to an action/account, duration and conditions. Its use requires the actual agent, resources and consent or represented enforcement; no label establishes sovereignty. Local meetings, delegated work and multiple settlements can then compose without prescribing an inevitable government form. Knowledge likewise needs actual people or durable material records, copying effort, location and loss. The corrected contradictory-trial records remain useful, while global 64-record eviction must not substitute for destruction of every information carrier. See [R09/R10/R23 and the construction review](open-construction.md).

## Proposed promotion criteria

These are **initial engineering targets to measure**, not achieved limits or empirical laws. Do not launch the next load tier until the previous one passes on the declared hardware and exact implementation. The subsequent simulation-only 64-tick profile does not satisfy these longer service/load gates.

| Gate                              | Concrete acceptance experiment                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reproducible baseline             | Record 1,200-person state/code/request hashes, active regions, observers and duration. Measure simulation lag, tick time, event-loop delay, peak total memory, decoded/wire bytes separately, checkpoint time, database/WAL/scratch bytes and recovery correctness.                                                                                                                                 |
| Bounded observation               | Start with ≤64 KiB overview responses, ≤256 returned markers/page, ≤1 MiB decoded detail pages and ≤128 KiB routine deltas. Hold a local view fixed while multiplying distant population by ten: local payload and projection work must stay within 10%, with stable IDs and no missing local travelers. Treat these budgets as proposals requiring the new projection protocol.                    |
| Service headroom                  | At the current host limit, target ≤70% steady and ≤85% peak accounted memory, including **one** allowed backup/preflight overlap. Free storage must exceed the next operation's uncompressed output, worst observed WAL/scratch growth and a reserve of at least 20% of volume capacity. If it cannot fit, move that operation off-host or resize explicitly.                                       |
| Throughput and lifecycle          | In a 30-minute disposable-host run, target mean complete-tick cost ≤175 ms against the 250 ms wall budget, p99 loop delay <100 ms and health latency <1 s; lag must not trend upward. Test slow observers, reconnects, checkpointing and interrupted/repeated handover with bounded queues and the same sole writer.                                                                                |
| Scaling ladder                    | After indexing, compare 1,200 →12,000 →120,000 synthetic individuals, then a larger target only if measured. Vary dense/sparse layouts, one large civilization/many smaller ones, active region count, structures, mature memories and 0/1/20/100 observers independently. Separate simulation-only, observer-only and lifecycle-overlap runs; report scaling curves, not one headline population.  |
| Conservative partition/refinement | Two adjacent regions versus one combined domain; crossing movement, shared water/heat, illness, inventory and record exchange. Require no negative/duplicated pools, exact integer census/event identities, declared element/energy tolerances and reproducible ordering. For genuinely approximate coarse dynamics, preregister distribution/error bounds instead of claiming bitwise equivalence. |
| Open-ended behavior               | Submit an unlisted arrangement/operation and a new combination of scoped commitments. Changing names leaves physical results unchanged; rebuilding/copying pays actual costs; failure conserves material; access and knowledge follow carriers; revocation, absence or interrupted delivery changes what can occur. Unsupported interactions remain explicit.                                       |

The immediate sequence is **bounded published views and operational headroom → measured inner-loop allocation fixes, local indexes and incremental persistence → conservative regional execution → validated coarse/fine representations and broader operations**. More RAM or a successful 1,200-person renewal buys capacity; it does not establish that the architecture supports massive civilizations or unforeseen creations.

**Housing is not a direct conception or delivery gate.** Local shelter adds up to four points to satisfaction, improves rest and changes thermal demand; those can affect health/happiness indirectly. The 365-day cooldown plus 270-day gestation gives a minimum 635 days between births for a continuing pair. These are chosen demographic rules, not measured biological constants.

Partner ID determines the gestator, and conception does not recheck pair distance, partner health or an expressed reproductive intention. Overdue pregnancies wait for delivery's resource gates rather than following a modeled obstetric outcome. These are explicit abstraction limits; change them through a separate reviewed demographic model, preserving existing pregnancies and ancestry.

## Remaining shelter and generation limits

- **Food selection can exclude all new non-build assembly choices.** [citizens.ts](../../src/simulation/citizens.ts), lines 255–270 and 321–329, reuses one random `chance`. Below the seasonal reserve target, a successful food assignment returns for `chance < 0.65` (0.85 under nourish). Outside build focus, later assembly requires `chance < 0.4 × disposition`; [cognition.ts](../../src/simulation/cognition.ts), line 173, bounds disposition at 0.55–1.55, so its threshold is at most **0.62**. All such assembly choices have already returned. Existing builders can continue; a failed food search or satisfied reserve target changes the condition. The seasonal target can reach **518.58 kg/person**, or **155.57 t for 300**, above the renewal's initial 144 t. This conditional exclusion needs a separate action-selection test; fixing candidate eligibility alone does not fix labor allocation. Do not change random draws without checking shared RNG ordering and intended priorities.
- **Selection is not proportional to unmet needs.** `designScore` always weights cover/storage/work by 8/6/2, subtracting mass×0.008. The planner's unmet-need checks filter candidates but do not alter that ranking. One active autonomous project and the 48-tick interval limit starts; food gathering, repair, sleep and experiments compete for labor. The autonomous planner lacks the food-reserve veto applied to advised assemblies. Whether any of these currently binds needs a dated reason/count trace, not inference from housing alone.
- **Sites have a fixed spatial ceiling.** Requests use the annulus 1.5<distance<6 coordinate cells and reject centers within 1.8 cells of any structure, including ruins. For an integer-centered, entirely available land grid, there are 100 candidate centers and at most **28** mutually permitted centers, calculated by row-mask enumeration. Existing ruins, water, ownership and reachability reduce that number. This is not a proof that housing 300 is impossible: it requires an average **18.21 m² covered area per site** if every site serves housing at full condition. It is a real constraint on many small creations and later growth.
- **Capacity differs from usable shelter.** [housing](../../src/simulation/world.ts) multiplies capacity by condition; night assignments in [citizens.ts](../../src/simulation/citizens.ts), line 228 onward, use floored unadjusted capacity, while local protection uses capacity divided by nearby people. Shelter selection takes the first qualifying structure, not the nearest reachable one; a failed path does not try another shelter in that decision. The 1.7 m²/person allowance and roof threshold at 1.6 m are model assumptions, not a complete dwelling standard. Rain exclusion, ventilation, internal access and occupancy geometry are incomplete.
- **Finite material is necessary, not sufficient.** The model requires **510 m²** covered area for 300 full-condition places. At its 25 mm minimum thickness and 520 kg/m³ wood density, roofs alone would consume at least **6.63 t wood**, before supports and losses. The reported renewal began with 24 t wood/community plus retained stock; ordinary founding provides 3.6 t. Neither stock figure proves load support, useful geometry, available labor or replacement supply. Preserve the distinction between these two starting conditions.
- **Infant development and care remain substantial missing connections.** Birth currently places the child at the camp and can debit camp stocks/water while the gestator is elsewhere. Newborns can take rations, drink, walk at the generic child factor and select rest/social tasks without a caregiver transfer. Meals retain 35% until body matter reaches 18 kg, regardless of age. Under steady baseline feeding, `1.15×24/48×0.35 = 0.20125 kg/day` implies roughly **80 days from 2 to 18 kg represented tissue**; this is arithmetic, not an executed survival trial or a complete anatomical weight. Age-dependent growth, feeding/carrying, protection and care labor need connected budgets before claiming realistic later generations.

The geometry/search limits still permit arbitrary arrangements within five materials and 32 axis-aligned parts. Recipes should remain evidence records after invention. A shortage should prompt inspection of feasible operations, knowledge carriers, allocation and sites; neither a named house nor a desired birth count supplies missing matter or work.

## Small causal acceptance experiments, proposed only

1. **Planner alternative:** two actual evaluated, remembered, stable designs; the affordable higher-ranked one exceeds 1,600 kg, the other does not. First verify a rejected request leaves serialized world/RNG/next ID unchanged. Then verify planning starts exactly the eligible alternative, debits its cost once and leaves construction unfinished. Add all-blocked and changed-affordability cases; no broad simulation is needed.
2. **Work-choice exclusion:** keep immediate bodily needs satisfied, the reserve target unmet, food search feasible and an active project reachable. Trace the same-draw food/assembly eligibility under balance and build. A later correction should retain urgent nourishment while giving feasible non-emergency work its declared opportunity; specify selection and RNG semantics before changing them.
3. **Gestation and delivery:** tiny balanced fixtures immediately before/on the due daily boundary; vary body and accessible water around the current thresholds. Assert birth timing, parent IDs and matter/element conservation. Test separation from camp as a documented current limitation and define local carrier behavior before changing it. The existing `planet.test.ts` forces a pregnancy due and checks one birth; it does not establish natural conception or child survival.
4. **Usable shelter:** a few people, two different-distance shelters, one blocked route, and reduced condition. Measure assignments, occupancy, heat expenditure and reachable food/water. Require one explicit capacity convention and a reachable fallback; no extra roof or supplies appear.
5. **Growth and care:** a newborn, carers and fixed finite supplies in a small local fixture. Record every food/water transfer, tissue growth, carrying and caregiver work lost. Establish age/growth and care assumptions before adding acceptance bounds; survival without represented delivery must not silently substitute for care.

Prioritize experiment 1 for the next narrow hotfix. The other experiments prepare separate model work; births now occurring do not justify either a survival guarantee or an emergency demographic intervention.

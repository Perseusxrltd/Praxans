# Open construction and knowledge — 8 October 2026

**A design should describe an arrangement of material, and a recipe should record a practice someone discovered. Neither should grant an effect or unlock a law.** The current implementation takes a useful first step: `Design.name` does not determine strength, shelter or storage, and external proposals can supply their own cuboids without choosing a building type. It does not yet represent everything physically possible from found material.

This review extends the [scale audit](scale-audit.md), [300-person founding critique](300-person-founding.md) and [observer architecture](rendering-review.md). It inspects `economy.ts`, `laws.ts`, `cognition.ts`, the construction paths in `citizens.ts` and `engine.ts`, and the action/schema/evaluation interfaces. The [machine-readable record](construction-review.json) preserves fingerprints and a reproduced enclosure defect. No simulation or live state was changed.

This is the initial audit. The later [connected-model review](connected-model-review.md) verifies the enclosure correction and inspects the general component generator that replaced the post/slab prior. The [release coherence review](release-coherence-review.md) subsequently verifies separate notes for contradictory results and different research methods, correcting the earlier construction-method overwrite. The older findings remain below with their original source context; physical stock placement, prediction/evidence and carrier limits remain open.

## Foundations and the present boundary

Vitruvius's distinction between supporting ground and a structure's foundations, and Agricola's accounts of connected passages, supports, air, drainage and removed rock (`A11`, `A12`), supply useful questions about arrangement and access. Xenophon's household accounting (`A04`) asks where material is kept and how it is retrieved. These are consulted premodern descriptions and prescriptions, not validated numerical strength laws. The enclosure counterexample below follows directly from geometry and inspected code; it needs no appeal to historical authority.

The practical requirement is open composition within explicitly represented interactions. A finite computational model cannot promise every natural possibility, molecular realism or arbitrary accuracy. It can avoid named unlocks, expose unsupported interactions, and extend a shared material/geometry model when evidence justifies the next operation. A recipe becomes a dated, revisable account of inputs, arrangements, work, conditions and observed outcomes after a trial or invention. A new name or copied recipe changes neither matter nor physical capability.

The external HTTP/MCP evaluation route is explicitly described as a free, read-only static estimate. Keep that fact visible in the information contract: an external advisor can consult a model, while an inhabitant's claimed experimental evidence must say what was actually done. The current source already labels sample results as idealized static estimates; downstream records and decisions should retain that distinction rather than treating every returned quantity as measured fact. A theory can predict beyond direct observation, but needs provenance, scope and possible error.

The five material categories are also effective assumptions: for example, all wood shares one density and strength, and generic clay has no separate wet, dried or fired state. A metal element in the element catalog does not establish workable metal, a furnace or a material transformation. Any extension must connect actual feedstock, processing, waste, heat/work and resulting properties.

Candidate `R22` is a bounded correction: compute a connected void/boundary representation from cuboid edges and count only the appropriate contained region. A continuous open-top bin can have granular storage volume without being rainproof, airtight or watertight. Distinguish those affordances instead of making one scalar promise all of them. Test a connected box, detached walls, a doorway/slit and a removed wall. Translate or rename a design without changing its result. A tessellation or flood-fill implementation must respect actual gaps and documented tolerance; it must not close a gap merely because the grid is coarse.

Do not silently reinterpret the capacity of saved structures during activation. An evaluator correction changes their physical behavior and can affect survival: version the law, recompute deliberately, preserve IDs/material/history, and report the resulting capacity changes. No new food or replacement wall is implied.

## Next bounded mechanism: operations, trials and descriptive plans

Candidate `R23` separates the physical extension from how inhabitants learn about it. Begin with the existing assembly model and one useful operation, rather than adding an entire catalog of machines:

1. **Track one material item or stock allocation through placement and a trial.** Specify its source, mass, geometry, work, destination and any recoverable waste. Propose an explicit arrangement, not a requested effect such as “make a warm house.” An invalid or unsupported arrangement produces a specific failure or limitation.
2. **Record prediction and observation separately.** A sample can inform a material estimate. A full-size load or containment trial has its actual mass, span, load, duration and outcome. Predicted full-scale performance is identified as inference. One successful use does not establish unlimited load, impermeability or long-term endurance.
3. **Select from local problems and observations.** Compare reducing a food-storage loss, providing cover, producing a work surface and learning an uncertain material property. Keep their separate expected effects and costs; permit different people to favor different experiments. Remove the universal geometry score as a physical truth. A bounded candidate generator can still have declared search biases and limited compute.
4. **Write a descriptive plan after evidence.** Store provenance-bearing notes or a holder's memory of the operation, materials, dimensions, conditions, failure modes and uncertainty. Copying or teaching the note requires a carrier and contact. No technology flag appears. Rebuilding still pays all material/work costs and can fail under changed conditions.
5. **Make forgetting local.** A shared index can evict a cached summary, but must not erase another person's still-retained knowledge. Keep a record while an actual holder or represented durable carrier owns it; a record's survival alone does not make it known to everyone. Address bounded storage explicitly rather than creating a permanent universal library.

The smallest discriminating checks are a renamed design, two geometries with different physical outcomes, a tiny material sample versus a full-size trial, changed loading after a successful trial, and a record held by one person when a community cache evicts its summary. Compare material and work ledgers, stated evidence and each person's access. These checks are proposed; only the two enclosure fixtures above were executed here.

Rotatable components, joining, cutting, shaping, heating and connected underground structures remain separate extensions of the same operation/quantity contract. They should not be introduced as compulsory historical stages. `R13`–`R16` supply units, finite depth reservoirs, excavation and conservative refinement; `R09` supplies knowledge carriers. Browser close views must show the actual supported geometry at stated scale, with unknown or unrepresented depth kept distinct from existing cavities.

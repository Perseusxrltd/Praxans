## Immediate findings

### 1. Diagnose the domain before attributing a camp's failure

**Initial code observation:** the original isolated mode of `scripts/renewal-trials.ts` keeps the community's single 32 × 32 region, removes other communities and out-of-region objects, proportionally reduces shared atmospheric stocks, and rebuilds its accounting baseline. It correctly labels this a sealed-edge diagnostic. A community near an edge loses part of its actual 120 m habitat. A larger domain also changes weather exchange and the sequence of random draws; it is not simply the same experiment with extra food. The later full-catchment option is recorded in the update below.

**Arithmetic:** a continuous disk of radius 120 m has 4.524 ha. On the current 10 m lattice, a complete disk centered on an integer cell contains **441 cell centers**, or 4.41 ha of cell footprints, before water, shore, ownership, and reachability exclusions. Thirty-two adults correspond to roughly **707 people per km² of the ideal disk**. This is a catchment ratio, not an estimate of actual regional population density or a universal carrying-capacity limit.

**Test:** report requested, represented, land, restored, observed, and reachable cells separately, together with food/plant mass by distance band. Compare a home-region diagnostic with a domain containing the complete catchment and a weather/transport buffer, using only disposable copies. Record forcing and RNG differences. First determine whether failure persists with the relevant habitat represented; do not interpret a one-region ranking as a whole-world forecast. Queue `R01`.

### 2. A starting reserve can conceal an annual deficit

**Historical starting point:** Xenophon distinguishes grain rooms from wine storage and sets apart monthly and yearly provisions (_Oeconomicus_ IX, `A04`). The _Arthashastra_ distinguishes inflows, loans, processing, reserves, and replacement of old stores (II.15, `A08`). These are evidence that authors reasoned about time, condition, and accounts; they do not justify copying a universal reserve fraction or their social hierarchies.

**Code observation:** food enters camp stock, personal provisions, cargo, bodies, newborns, experiments, construction, and trade. Death and spoilage return material. The trial's `foodKg` measures camp stock only; `harvestKg` is cumulative and includes earlier history unless differenced. Neither alone measures current production or consumption. Up to 3 kg per inhabitant can be held as separate rations. The new `edibleReserves` helper already makes this distinction for some uses.

**Test:** reconcile a daily edible-food account: opening accessible reserves + harvest + imports − meals − thermal/ice fuel − spoilage − exports − material allocated to growth/birth/other uses = closing reserves, including carried food. Mark transfers between these pools rather than double-counting them. Report unmet needs, return per gathering hour, and the share of consumption funded by the original intervention. Compare successive growing and lean seasons before increasing startup supplies. Queue `R02`.

### 3. Storage loss needs conditions, not an unconditional clock

**Historical observation:** Theophrastus distinguishes viability from food storage and notes differences by seed kind, moisture, and locality (_Enquiry into Plants_ VIII.10–11, `A02`). His explanation that decaying seeds engender creatures is not accepted here; pests need living sources. His exceptional longevity reports are reports, not calibrated survival curves.

**Measured/compiled check:** FAO's _Grain Storage Techniques_ (`M03`) describes interacting grain condition, water availability, temperature, oxygen, and organisms. Its example contrasts maize at 15% moisture: relatively safe storage for a year at 15°C versus substantial mould damage within three months at 30°C. This does not establish equivalent lifetimes for every stored food. Cooling is also not an absolute guarantee against mould.

**Initial code observation:** `decayStocks` imposed `0.004 + 0.004 * warmth * (0.3 + damp)` per day on food. The temperature-independent 0.004 floor alone leaves only 48.7% after 180 days, with a 173-day half-life, even in protected frozen storage. At 20°C the whole rate spans 0.0052–0.0092/day as dampness varies. The reserve planner assumes 0.006/day and a winter demand of 1.6 kg/person/day; its 180-day maximum is **518.58 kg/person**, not 288 kg.

**Candidate update reported during this batch:** the implementation collaborator now applies the existing temperature factor to the entire biological food-loss rate, preserving the old 20°C value and finite cold loss. **Q10 = 2, its lower clamp, and the rate coefficient remain effective model assumptions.** This research does not claim the candidate is deployed or seasonally validated. A cold/warm conservation regression and existing reruns are the immediate evidence. Later refinement can distinguish moisture, exposure, damaged tissue, and pest access through physical state, without item recipes. Queue `R03`.

### 4. Faster walking can skip the information along a journey

**Historical starting point:** Hippocratic _Airs, Waters, Places_ 1 (`A03`) asks a visitor to attend to seasons, winds, water, aspect, and local habits. Xenophon XVI (`A04`) reasons from visible crops and neighboring land, while also making generalizations that need checking.

**Code observation:** `walkPath` can traverse many cells in a 15-minute tick at an effective 1,800 m/hour before fatigue/terrain reductions. `updateMind` perceives only hourly, from the current position before movement. Resources beside intermediate cells can remain unknown although a person walked past them. Raising speed alone does not increase useful geographical knowledge.

**Test:** put a source beside the middle of a route, outside sensory range of both endpoints. Compare knowledge after one integrated trip and after a subdivided trip. Collect bounded observations along physically crossed positions, with light/attention limits, and verify that an untraveled source is still unknown. Check runtime cost and the 48-place capacity together. Queue `R04`.

### 5. Distance-only gathering can repeatedly choose poor returns

**Code observation:** `gatherTask` searches within 120 m of home; food requires a remembered value above 2 and an observation less than seven days old. Unknown-site exploration uses a 100 m radius. Forty-eight place entries are retained by recency; ordinary observation can evict distant useful places. Gather candidates are ranked by distance rather than expected yield per total effort. A gathering task then needs 2.4 effective work-hours, even when a patch gives little. `teachNearby` teaches construction observations, not a shared map of resources.

**Measured comparison:** Pontzer et al. (`M02`) measured mean daily walking of 5.8 ± 1.7 km for women and 11.4 ± 2.1 km for men in a 30-adult Hadza sample over 11 days. These are travel distances, not radial catchments, and one population is not a universal human baseline. They show why 120 m should be described as an implementation restriction rather than a biological mobility limit.

**Test:** two already observed patches with different expected yields should allow comparison of delivered food per outbound + work + return hour. Hide the better patch's observation to verify that the decision does not consult its actual unseen stock. Test memory expiry, depleted patches, scarce water, a blocked return route, and the effect of repeatedly reobserving home. Try salience and uncertainty before simply enlarging memory; make expanding search depend on return-time and ration budgets. Queue `R05`.

### 6. An old planet is not automatically mature woodland

**Historical starting point:** Theophrastus distinguishes forms of growth, seed behavior, locality, and preservation (`A02`); Aristotle asks which processes are shared and which differ between organisms (_Parts of Animals_ I.1, `A01`). Functional distinctions are a useful starting point. Neither author supplies the simulation's biomass coefficients.

**Units:** `plant.carbon` is a dry organic equivalent pool, not elemental carbon. It must not be multiplied or divided by the forest-carbon fraction as though it were kg C. In the renewal request, 48 kg/cell is **4.8 t organic equivalent/ha**, approximately 4.992 t/ha including its initial mineral component. It applies only where a layer is empty and a template exists; existing smaller plants are retained.

**Code observation:** the main-layer capacity is `160 / (1 + woodiness)` kg/cell: about 8.2–8.9 t organic equivalent/ha at woodiness 0.80–0.96, versus about 14.5 t/ha at woodiness 0.10. Root allocation affects turnover but is not a separate reserve pool. Increasing the bulk pool changes food, light interception, mineral demand, and growth together. A mass increase is therefore not a clean test of mature structural wood.

**Compiled bounds:** visually checked IPCC 2006 tables (`M01`) give temperate continental Asia/Europe stands older than 20 years a default **120 t dry matter/ha**, with a 20–320 range; younger stands have a 20 t/ha default. Boreal forest spans 10–90 t/ha, and older boreal tundra woodland 15–20 t/ha. These defaults are broad comparisons, not site-specific targets or proof that every old landscape must be dense forest. The current values describe sparse or young vegetation more readily than typical mature temperate woodland.

IPCC Table 4.9 separately gives temperate continental net aboveground biomass growth of 4 t dry matter/ha/year, with an older-stand range of 0.5–7.5. **That is not edible production or total NPP.** Standing biomass, annual tissue production, human-edible fraction, sustainable removal, and accessible harvest must be measured separately.

**Test:** first partition measured growth, respiration, litter loss, seed investment, and harvest by functional tissue/season. A later bounded change could introduce structural tissue and living reserve pools by conserving each existing plant's total mass. Shade, respiration, reproduction, and regrowth must depend on the appropriate pools. Removing leaves must not consume the entire trunk proportionally; a surviving root reserve must not regrow without water, nutrients, and paid respiration. Queue `R06`.

## Later 300-adult spatial diagnostic on 8 October 2026

This is a **causal hypothesis and implementation in progress, pending comparison**. Distribution must conserve actual transfers and represent stock locations or hauling; choosing arbitrary cool destinations would not resolve the physical model. `R24` and the [founding review's later update](300-person-founding.md#later-update-spatial-concentration-and-heat) preserve the next discriminating checks.

# Investigating the stopped world

The format-7 / biosphere-1.1 process halted at tick 20276 after biochemical energy validation failed. Its last valid persisted checkpoint is **20244**. Restarting the same binary repeats the failure; a healthy provider deployment status does not mean its simulation is healthy.

The consistent backup `before-recovery-20261008.sqlite` has SHA-256 `a8bbb992dfbfbc5866490479d71294e787e088f138108b7e0473d577b2dbeeed`. It retains four communities and their history, but no living people or animal cohorts. **132 plant cohorts remain**, totaling 6.331892 kg of organic tissue. The claim that every living organism was dead was therefore inaccurate. These observations concern the 38 materialized regions, not a fully simulated planetary biosphere.

## Two connected failures

The biochemical guard's tolerance was proportional to *remaining chemical energy*, including organic remains and stored material. The large photosynthesis and respiration counters accumulated rounding error while this remaining pool shrank. At the saved checkpoint, approximately 50.5 billion kJ had been released; the mismatch was **0.878396 kJ**, just below the old **0.903273 kJ** tolerance. Further ordinary transfers crossed that shrinking threshold.

This numerical failure did not cause the preceding deaths. The old reduced weather model returned infrared energy from a surface-relative emitting temperature without balancing that atmospheric emitter's energy supply. In an isolated continuation, inhabited locations reached roughly 70–75°C, water and plant food deteriorated, and the original people died. Urgent eating could also fail to interrupt a task, and fractional or carried food was not consistently usable.

Recovery additionally exposed biological weaknesses: canopy size reduced small plants' light response twice, trapping seedlings below viable growth, and leaf turnover was applied to structural wood. Healthy trunks effectively inherited a 44-day leaf half-life. There was no dormant propagule reservoir.

## The correction

- Keep the original save, clock debt, identities, inventories and history. Empty seed banks start at the intervention; no missing ancestors, seeds, people or animals are fabricated.
- Persist compensated accumulation for biochemical energy and atmospheric transfers. Judge biochemical numerical error against the energy processed; tests still reject significant new violations.
- Fund returned atmospheric radiation from absorbed infrared and shortwave energy. Distinguish frozen soil/lake water from snowfall, include frozen water's heat capacity, and let foliage mask ground snow.
- Couple detailed regions to 36 equal-area planetary thermal bands. Stellar forcing, outgoing radiation, finite heat storage and conservative meridional heat exchange evolve those reservoirs. Local sensible heat is withdrawn from or returned to them. Detailed areas are excluded from the coarse radiative forcing. The parameters are explicit effective Earth-analogue assumptions, not a fluid solver or a complete planetary thermodynamic model.
- Separate ambient leaf light from the actual intercepted energy budget, slow structural wood turnover, and reduce cold-season metabolism. Growth still needs real carbon, water, nutrients and light.
- Transfer parent-funded seeds/spores into bounded dormant cohorts. Dormancy consumes finite reserves; suitable temperature, moisture, light, oxygen and a free niche permit germination. Nonviable propagules become litter. Existing birds and local plant dispersal carry these cohorts.
- Let urgent needs interrupt work and make available food usable. A dead community's owner can explicitly begin a new community in wilderness while preserving the old one; the old keys are retired.

## Why the longer checks matter

An initial balanced radiation candidate passed a short historical survival continuation but froze during a longer ecological test. A second candidate overheated. Distinguishing frozen ground from snow and adding atmospheric shortwave absorption passed the first 120 days, but the **400-day** test exposed an isolated-region winter collapse to **−95°C**. This is why the regional heat connection was added instead of accepting a summer-only result or imposing a temperature floor.

With planetary heat exchange, the same 400-day trial stayed between **−11.92°C and 27.49°C**, retaining about **4,246 kg** of growing plant tissue from an initial **39,226 kg**. This establishes surviving producers into the next spring under these conditions. The large seasonal loss, provisional biological rates, and absence of a multi-generation ecological/demographic ensemble remain explicit limitations. It is not evidence of an eternal equilibrium or guaranteed civilization survival.

Permanent tests exercise seed-funded recovery after parental death, dry dormancy, finite reserves, failed viability, small-plant growth, woody turnover, radiative funding, regional heat exchange, planetary energy accounting, numerical accumulation and restart continuity. An actually sterile region needs immigration or another represented source of life; the simulation does not create organisms to repair a population graph.

Final release verification and provider status belong in the [validation index](README.md) and the release record. Historical rejected candidates are not evidence that the final model is deployed.

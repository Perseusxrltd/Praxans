# Voluntary local food handoff

**Validated candidate, 2026-10-09.** Physical format **14 / biosphere-1.8**, storage **2**. Publication has not yet occurred. The existing world remains on `region-storage-20261009-1`. The exact candidate runtime SHA-256 is `42900a5d86c887049e791ff96b7866f775e638f26b50aeac9b612215c46bb273`; [measured evidence](../validation/local-food-handoff-2026-10-09.json) separates local checks from publication.

An infant could ingest its own provisions but could not receive food held by a nearby adult. The [bounded diagnostic](../research/infant-food-access-2026-10-09.md) measured that access gap without injury over four hours; it did not measure a historical death share. The [historical collapse](../research/community-collapse-2026-10-08.md), including the exactly reproduced first infant's cold death and the ration-order artifact, remains separate evidence.

People can now choose to spend delivery work handing their own food to someone in their occupied cell. A new decision performs no work until the next funded interval. Meals and ration pickup precede the common holder snapshot; all donor debits precede recipient credits. Donors retain private custody until an actual transfer. Changing location, walking, entering a journey, death or task replacement prevents delivery. Kinship and community identity supply no special physical permission. Food enters the recipient's provisions, with normal ingestion only in a subsequent interval.

Existing social disposition and urgent bodily needs govern the helper's choice. A coarse hunger/empty-bundle proxy supplies the local opportunity; no other person's private intake or reserve is inspected. Handling is capped at 2 kg per funded work-hour, with a voluntary target of 0.05 kg per kg of represented body, capped at the existing 3 kg ration target. These are explicit approximations. The mechanism does not represent recipient consent, lactation, food preparation or assisted swallowing, and does not calibrate childhood growth. A delivery can reinforce practiced handling; it grants no nutritional success, food, health or completed work for free.

Migration **014-performed-local-food-handoff** changes only version labels at its boundary and records the intervention. Existing task objects, partial covering work, body compartments, provisions, cargo, caravan goods, terrain, RNG, clock, ownership and history remain exact. No communities are restored and no historical deaths are rewritten. The observer names the activity and reports kilograms actually handed over.

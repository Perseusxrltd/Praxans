A clamped concave quadratic reaches zero slope at the declared **18-year maturity**. Ordinary capacity gain and deficit recovery share a finite final gap; recovery uses a **90-day time constant**. A mature person can rebuild structure actually lost, while an oversized legacy child keeps its existing body. No target grants matter, compulsory growth or retrospective development.

Those rates and the curve are transparent model choices, not fitted anatomical measurements. Sensitivity controls vary maturity across 12/18/24 years and structural recovery across 30/90/180 days. Childhood mobility, food preparation, lactation, assisted swallowing, gestational energy, oxygen contention and body composition remain separate limitations.

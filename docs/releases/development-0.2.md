# Development independent of heating

**Candidate; publication pending.** Physical format **15 / biosphere-1.9**, storage **2**. This correction follows the [historical collapse study](../research/community-collapse-2026-10-08.md), the [measured food day](../research/food-flux-day-2026-10-09.md) and the [developmental review](../research/childhood-retention-budget-2026-10-09.md). None apportions every historical death or proves seasonal survival.

The old retention allowance increased when a person burned more food for cold, active work or ice melting. Structural growth had no developmental clock: an infant could approach the adult body reference within months. The new allowance uses at most the resting-maintenance equivalent of actual food oxidation. Unmet maintenance, thermal deficit or excess heat still prevents retention, and retained tissue still debits actual unoxidized intake. Processing remains included in the coarse maintenance account; the inherited 35% convention is a throughput bound, not a measured synthesis cost.

Structural capacity now changes with age using the shared orbital-year duration. The existing **2 and 18 kg dry-equivalent total-body references** become **1.8 and 16.2 kg structural references** after excluding the 10% reserve subset. A clamped concave quadratic reaches zero slope at the declared **18-year maturity**. Ordinary capacity gain and deficit recovery share a finite final gap; recovery uses a **90-day time constant**. A mature person can rebuild structure actually lost, while an oversized legacy child keeps its existing body. No target grants matter, compulsory growth or retrospective development.

Those rates and the curve are transparent model choices, not fitted anatomical measurements. Sensitivity controls vary maturity across 12/18/24 years and structural recovery across 30/90/180 days. Childhood mobility, food preparation, lactation, assisted swallowing, gestational energy, oxygen contention and body composition remain separate limitations.

Migration **015-age-bounded-structural-growth** changes only the two version labels at its boundary. It preserves every age, body compartment, last measured interval, task, inventory, terrain, RNG, clock, owner and historical record. The developmental budget is transient; no new per-person saved field or meal journal is introduced.
